# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## What we expect

- Welcoming, patient and respectful communication.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and giving it kindly.
- Focusing on what is best for people using this package.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers using
[GitHub's private vulnerability and abuse reporting](https://github.com/tickerfinance/market-hours/security/advisories/new),
or by reporting the content directly to GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed
promptly.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject any contribution, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor, for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
